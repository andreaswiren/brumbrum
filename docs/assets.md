# Assets

Runtime assets live in `apps/client/public/assets`. They are bundled locally; gameplay does not depend on an asset CDN.

- Poly Haven CC0: forest ground, forest sand, pine bark, pine sapling, and partly cloudy HDR sky. `assets/sources.json` records original URLs and modifications.
- domasx2 CC0 engine loop: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops. Original WAV and provenance/license are in `public/assets/audio`; runtime normalizes and pitch-shifts it, mixed with combustion synthesis.
- Quaternius human distributed by Mesh2Motion, CC0: continuous anatomically proportioned mesh with 66 authored joints. Source archive, license evidence and modifications are in `assets/human-rider-source.json`; the supplied license is copied beside `human-rider.glb`.
- Motorcycle, ATV, monster truck and snowmobile geometry, protective gear, grass, fallback boulders and snow detail are generated by project code. Imported character joints follow riding and ragdoll proxies.
- Six textured KayKit rock meshes from the supplied local asset library, CC0, with gray material tuning and terrain embedding. Provenance: `assets/kaykit-rocks.json`; license: `assets/licenses/KayKit-CC0.txt`; rebuild: `node tools/assets/import-local-rocks.mjs`.
- Additional full/young Poly Haven pines with near/far variants and a KayKit snag using photographic bark. Provenance and local source paths: `assets/forest-extras-source.json`; rebuild: `node tools/assets/prepare-forest-extras.mjs`.

The source pine is optimized into near/far GLBs with embedded textures. Rebuild downloaded assets with `node tools/assets/fetch-assets.mjs`, then `node tools/assets/optimize-pine.mjs`. Raw downloads stay in ignored `.asset-cache`, outside the production bundle. The human GLB is copied from the licensed local Mesh2Motion source archive; its source location is recorded separately.

Mesh2Motion supplies the human mesh and skeleton through the local archive. Its app is not a runtime dependency.

Babylon core/loaders/materials use their package licenses; Havok WASM, Vite, TypeScript, Vitest and glTF optimization tools retain theirs. Google Fonts serves Barlow/Barlow Condensed with system font fallbacks. Gameplay works without that font service.
